Powerful Features

Everything you need to run your library smoothly.

Ditch the notebooks and excel sheets. Automate your daily operations and focus on providing a great study environment for your students.

Visual Seat Management

Map out your library's seating. See which seats are occupied, reserved, or available in real-time.

Student Profiles

Keep a digital record of all members. Store contact details, ID proofs, and historical data securely.

Shift Management

Create multiple shifts (Morning, Evening, Night). Allocate seats to different students for different timeslots.

Fee & Payment Tracking

Never miss a payment. Track monthly fees, issue digital receipts, and see pending dues at a glance.

Waitlist System

Library full? Add interested students to a digital waitlist and contact them instantly when a seat opens up.

Finance & Analytics

View your monthly revenue, occupancy rates, and growth trends with beautiful, easy-to-read charts.
